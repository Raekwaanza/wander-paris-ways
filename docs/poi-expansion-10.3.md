# Step 10.3 — outer Paris POI expansion research

## Method and scope

The baseline was measured from `CURATED_POIS` before editing: 66 records, with no records assigned
to the 12th, 16th, 17th, or 18th arrondissements. Each addition was checked against a City of
Paris, official institution, national museum, or other institutional page. Research focused on
durable identity, history, architecture, access, and location; runtime prose is original and omits
hours, prices, exhibitions, and other fragile details. Coordinates represent public gates,
forecourts, paths, or short-street encounters rather than building or landscape centroids.

No runtime provenance, category, selectable interest, dependency, or application logic changed.

## Editorial and geographic review

### Large features

The Coulée verte, Parc de Bercy, Lac Daumesnil/Bois de Vincennes, Jardin du Ranelagh, Serres
d'Auteuil, and Promenade Pereire use named gates or representative public path segments. No point
claims to represent an entire bois, lake, linear promenade, or large park.

### Access and private-space caveats

Museums and institutional interiors may require admission. The Cité des Fleurs is anchored at its
gate because passage can be restricted; Villa Léandre is public but residential. Gardens follow
municipal access rules, and religious interiors follow active worship use. The dataset never
promises entry or a walk-through where it cannot be supported.

### Near-coordinate review

Pairs under or close to 50 m were calculated after insertion. None were found. Several deliberate
neighborhood pairs remain within a few hundred metres—Aligre/Square Trousseau,
Balzac/Rue Berton, and ICI/Square Léon—but each is a different encounter type and route evidence.
The 18th retains only four Montmartre/Lamarck records; La Chapelle and Goutte d'Or supply the other
four, avoiding artificial density around Place du Tertre.

### Score calibration

Only the city-defining Sacré-Cœur/Trocadéro encounters receive scenic or popularity 10. Hidden
scores are concentrated on genuinely tucked-away streets and gates. Local gardens, markets, and
institutions use mid-range scenic/historic values. Nature 10 is limited to the lakeside and
botanical garden; food 9–10 is limited to established markets. The new records therefore broaden
coverage without assigning universal landmark-level scores.

### Rejected candidates

- **Generic Bois de Vincennes / Bois de Boulogne:** a point centroid would misrepresent enormous landscapes.
- **Place du Tertre, Saint-Pierre, Clos Montmartre, Jardins Renoir, rue de l'Abreuvoir:** individually valid, but rejected to prevent a small Montmartre cluster dominating corridor evidence.
- **Palais de Tokyo / Musée d'Art Moderne pair:** rejected in favor of wider 16th-arrondissement distribution.
- **Fondation Louis Vuitton / Bagatelle:** strong but too detached from the selected walkable clusters for this batch.
- **Villa Montmorency / Villa des Ternes:** access semantics were not strong enough for a public encounter.
- **Cimetière de Picpus / Montmartre Cemetery:** cemetery access and a missing canonical encounter category made other researched candidates preferable.
- **Commercial cafés and bookshops:** no candidate combined sufficiently durable institutional evidence and geographic value to displace the selected markets and civic sites.

No existing POI was modified. Routing, ORS parameters, the 120 m corridor, scoring weights,
Scenic/Explorer selection, and Wander Preview remain unchanged.
